IR/DR Plan Methodology

Incident Response (NIST SP 800-61)

Incident response is the organized approach to addressing and managing a security breach or attack. The NIST framework defines 5 phases:

  1. Preparation — Establish policies, assemble the IR team, deploy detection tools, and train staff before an incident occurs.
  2. Detection & Analysis — Identify indicators of compromise (IOCs), scope the incident, and prioritize based on impact.
  3. Containment — Isolate affected systems to stop the spread. Short-term containment preserves forensic evidence; long-term containment restores a clean state.
  4. Eradication — Remove malware, close attack vectors, patch vulnerabilities, and eliminate the root cause.
  5. Recovery — Restore affected systems to normal operations, monitor for recurrence, and verify integrity.
  6. Post-Incident Review — Document lessons learned, update the IR plan, and improve defenses based on findings.

Disaster Recovery Methodology

Disaster recovery focuses on restoring IT systems and data after a disruptive event (natural disaster, ransomware, hardware failure). Key phases:

  1. Prevention — Reduce risk through redundancy, off-site backups, power protection, and documented continuity procedures.
  2. Detection — Automated monitoring and alerting to identify outages, data loss, or degraded service as early as possible.
  3. Recovery — Execute the recovery runbook: restore from backup, activate failover systems, and communicate status to stakeholders.
  4. Post-Recovery — Validate restored systems, document the event, update the DR plan, and conduct a retrospective.

Key Recovery Metrics

  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective) — The maximum acceptable length of time a system can be offline after an incident. Example: "4 hours."
  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective) — The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time. Example: "1 hour" means backups must be no older than 1 hour.

IR Team Roles

  • Incident Commander — Leads the response, makes escalation decisions.
  • Security Analyst — Investigates alerts, performs triage and forensics.
  • System Administrator — Executes containment and recovery actions on affected systems.
  • Communications Lead — Manages notifications to stakeholders, legal, and regulators.
  • Legal / Compliance — Advises on reporting obligations and evidence preservation.
